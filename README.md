### 💼 Freelancer Dashboard Management System — 🚧 Currently Building

A modern **freelancer management dashboard** designed to help freelancers organize and manage their work from a centralized workspace.

The system focuses on simplifying day-to-day freelance operations by bringing essential project and business management tools into one dashboard.

**Key Features:**

* 📊 Dashboard overview and analytics
* 👥 Client management
* 📁 Project management
* ✅ Task and progress tracking
* 💰 Income and expense tracking
* 🧾 Invoice management
* 📅 Deadlines and scheduling
* 🔔 Notifications and reminders
* 📈 Performance and business insights

**Status:** 🚧 Currently in Development

The website includes donation categories, mission information, community activities, videos, and Google Form integration for collecting donation details.

**Focus:** Frontend Development • Responsive UI • Form Integration • Netlify Deployment
